## Overview

This ongoing project examines publicly reported U.S. defense infrastructure investment in the Philippines, focusing on the location, purpose, and status of funding associated with EDCA sites. The dataset distinguishes program milestones, site allocations, and individual projects to reduce double counting. Current funding records cover 2022–2023. Source reconciliation and dashboard development remain in progress; recorded allocations should not be interpreted as verified expenditures.



## Background 

The Enhanced Defense Cooperation Agreement (EDCA) provides a framework for U.S. military access to agreed Philippine defense facilities. Infrastructure associated with these sites includes runway improvements,fuel storage, warehouses, and command-and-control facilities.

Understanding this investment requires more than collecting dollar amounts. Public reports describe program-wide funding announcements, allocations to individual sites, and specific construction projects. These figures can overlap, refer to different reporting dates, or represent different stages of funding and delivery. An announced allocation does not, by itself, establish that money has been spent or construction completed.

This project examines those distinctions to make reported investments easier to compare by location, purpose, and status. It also identifies where missing documentation or conflicting figures limit the conclusions that can be drawn.


## Limitations

- Recorded allocations and announcements do not establish actual spending or completed construction.
- The current dataset does not include contract-level spending data.
- Individual site allocation records cover five sites; they do not provide a complete funding picture for all nine EDCA locations.
- The five-site subtotal of `$86.17 million` has not been reconciled with the separately recorded approximately `$82 million` milestone.
- Source identifiers still need to be connected to complete citations.
- Dashboard development and source validation remain in progress.



## Data & Methodology

The current funding dataset contains 10 records dated 2022–2023. Each record includes a location, description, date, funding status,amount, source identifier, and notes.

Records distinguish program milestones, cumulative site allocations, and individual projects. The `count_in_site_total` field identifies which records are included in the five-site subtotal, helping prevent overlapping program and project amounts from being added together.

The dataset documents corrections and unresolved issues, including the Fort Magsaysay amount correction from `$114 million` to `$11.4 million`. Full source citations and reconciliation of reported totals remain in progress.
